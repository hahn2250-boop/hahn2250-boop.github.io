Privacy Policy

Packstart Poster · Effective date: November 11, 2026

Summary

Packstart Poster runs entirely on your own computer. The developer runs no server and never receives your data.

1. Data the app uses

  • TikTok account data (via Login Kit, scope user.info.basic): your open ID, display name and username, to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, to post on your behalf (scope video.publish).
  • Your videos, captions and hashtags that you planned for posting.

2. Where data is stored

Tokens, settings and a log of published posts are stored only in the app folder on your computer. Nothing is stored by the developer.

3. Who receives data

  • TikTok receives the videos and captions you publish, as part of the TikTok Content Posting API. TikTok's Privacy Policy applies.
  • Anthropic (optional): only if you enter your own Anthropic API key, the app sends the video name and its text transcript to the Claude API to suggest a caption. Videos are never sent. Without an API key, nothing is sent to Anthropic.

Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

4. Deleting your data

Revoke the app's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, then delete the app folder from your computer. This removes all locally stored tokens and logs.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and follows TikTok's minimum age requirements.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy: hahn2250@gmail.com
